Complete the Words — Pair Warm-Up

TOEFL iBT Preparation Course · Session 2 · Work with a partner (10 minutes)

Work with your partner

Discuss each sentence together and help each other find the missing letters. Each line is one missing letter: the number of lines tells you how long the word is. The first half of every word is given.

Round 1

Hint: “is ______ many countries” — after is you need a verb ending in -ing. Which word follows “Climate”? Look at the topic.

Round 2

Hint: look at the word classes. “Students who ___ well” needs a verb; “perform ___ on” needs a comparative; “can ___ more” needs a base verb; “___ easily” — look at the ending -ily.

Discuss with your partner

  • Which clues helped you most: the first words, grammar, or word endings?
  • Which blank was the hardest? Why?
