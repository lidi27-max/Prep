Complete the Words — Pair Practice: What Kind of Word?

TOEFL iBT Preparation Course · Session 2 · Work with your partner (about 10 minutes)

How to work with your partner

  1. Read the whole paragraph first. The first sentence is complete: use it to find the topic.
  2. For each gap, discuss together and choose the kind of word in the drop-down list.
  3. Then type the missing letters. Each box is one missing letter. Spelling counts.
  4. Press Check, talk about the gaps you missed and fix them.

Both of you discuss every gap. One of you types in this page; the other helps and checks the spelling.
