Plan a Post Together

Work as a group for about 8 minutes. One person shares the screen and types. You are planning, not writing the whole post.
Dr. Rivera
This week we are discussing public transportation in large cities. Some city governments are considering making buses and trains free for everyone, paid for through taxes. Others believe riders should keep paying for the service. I would like you to discuss whether you think public transportation should be free in large cities. Explain why you think so.
Lucas
I think it should be free. Free buses and trains would reduce traffic, because more people would leave their cars at home. It would also help families with low incomes, who spend a large part of their budget on getting to work.
Maya
I disagree. Public transportation needs money to stay clean and on time, and fares pay for part of that. If the service becomes free, cities may have to raise taxes or reduce the number of buses, and that would hurt the same people we want to help.

Step 3 · Check your plan

Sample plan (one possible answer):
I · I believe public transportation should be free only for students, seniors and low-income riders.
R · This is because these groups depend on buses and trains the most, and a discount for them helps without taking all the fare money away from the system.
R (connection) · I understand Maya's concern about taxes and service quality; however, a targeted program costs far less than free rides for everyone, and it still supports the families Lucas wants to help.
T · For these reasons, a targeted free pass is the fairest solution.
Different positions can earn a high score. What matters is a clear idea, a developed reason with a connection to the discussion, and a closing sentence.
