Your professor is teaching a class on business. Write a post responding to the professor's question.

In your response, you should do the following.

  • Express and support your opinion.
  • Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.

An effective response will contain at least 100 words.

DA
Dr. Amin
In recent years, many countries have moved toward a cashless economy, where digital transactions through credit cards, mobile payments, and online banking have replaced physical cash. Some believe this shift makes transactions more convenient and secure, while others argue that a cashless society has drawbacks, such as privacy concerns and the exclusion of those without digital access. To what extent do you believe that a cashless society is the best way forward?
P
Priya
A fully cashless society is the best way forward because it simplifies transactions and reduces crime. Digital payments are faster, more secure, and eliminate risks associated with carrying cash, such as theft or loss. Many countries, like Sweden, have already embraced digital payments with great success, making everyday transactions more efficient for both businesses and consumers.
D
David
Relying entirely on digital payments could create serious problems, particularly for people who lack access to banking services or who have poor digital literacy. I strongly believe that cash is still essential for those in rural areas. It is also useful for elderly populations and lower-income groups who may not have smartphones or credit cards. Additionally, a cashless society increases dependence on technology, which could be a problem during system failures or cyberattacks.
